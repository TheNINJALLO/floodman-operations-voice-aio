# Company questions during a call

Avery answers company questions before resuming the pending intake question.
An information-only question must not become a name, contact value or job
description. An explicit request to arrange an estimate still starts intake.
The estimate offer uses the existing approved business policy: free inspections
and consultations; recommendations and pricing depend on the property and work.
This wording does not promise a repair price or a completed booking.

The question detector accepts plural service words, conversational introductions
and an offer verb with an imperfect recognized onset. Telephone recognition may
lose "you" or hear "pre estimates". The original transcript remains unchanged;
the handler uses the offer topic without inventing personal details. Recognized
plural estimate/inspection/consultation words use the fast streaming path rather
than waiting for the secondary decoder's deadline.

Approved deterministic answers run first, then approved local knowledge and
bounded local generation. Unknown details still require team confirmation.
No question answer performs an external action or changes routing, emergency,
consent, DNC, quiet-hour, billing verification or notification policy. Imported
learning drafts need review and approval before becoming company knowledge.

Regression coverage includes the actual imperfect estimate inquiries, questions
at each intake stage, conversational introductions, explicit service requests,
contact data, and secondary-decoder latency decisions. The build also checks
the packaged image and inherited offline Asterisk transfer handoff.
