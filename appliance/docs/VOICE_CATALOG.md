# Local voice catalog

The call system offers 19 English Pocket TTS presets. All use the same pinned
English model and voice-state revision; no customer audio or cloud speech
provider is needed. Preview the introduction, caring reply, or company answer
on the Voice page before saving. A preview does not change the live setting.
The installed voice remains selected through restarts.

Voice samples and source attribution are published by Kyutai at
https://huggingface.co/kyutai/tts-voices and
https://github.com/kyutai-labs/pocket-tts.
