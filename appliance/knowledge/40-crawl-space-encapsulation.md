Floodman advertises crawl-space encapsulation and moisture control. Common observations include musty odor, humidity, condensation, wet soil, standing water, damaged insulation, visible growth, cold floors, wood deterioration, and prior failed encapsulation.

Projects may involve a vapor barrier, sealing, drainage, dehumidification, insulation decisions, cleanup, or remediation. Exact materials and equipment require inspection. Active or contaminated water should be handled as urgent water intake first.
