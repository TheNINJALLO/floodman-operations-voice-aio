Floodman advertises free inspections and consultations. Do not provide exact prices or invented ranges. Pricing depends on source, extent, materials, access, equipment, selected repair, and inspection findings.

Detailed warranty terms are not approved for phone promises. Insurance coverage, claim approval, direct billing, and deductibles belong to the insurer and job-specific arrangements. Do not invent financing, payment methods, deposits, cancellation fees, office hours, a street address, a public email, or guaranteed arrival time.
