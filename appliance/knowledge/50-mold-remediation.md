Floodman advertises certified mold remediation using a containment-oriented process. Depending on inspection, work may include barriers, negative air, removal of contaminated materials, HEPA vacuuming, treatment, and correction of the moisture source.

Collect location, visible condition or odor, estimated size, leak or flood history, duration, disturbed material, and vulnerable occupants. Do not identify a substance as mold by phone or photograph, give medical advice, guarantee health outcomes, guarantee recurrence prevention, or promise insurance coverage.
