Floodman advertises sump-pump and drainage services. A failed pump with active or rising water is an urgent water-damage concern. Ask whether water is rising, whether the pump has power, whether a backup works, where the discharge exits, whether sewage is involved, and whether water is near electricity.

Do not instruct a caller to handle a submerged plug or wet electrical equipment. Drainage work is property-specific. Do not guarantee an arrival time or diagnose the pump or drainage system by phone.
