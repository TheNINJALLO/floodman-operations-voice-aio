# Advertised response

Floodman advertises 24/7 water-damage response for burst pipes, failed water heaters, sump-pump failures, flooded basements, leaks, and other water intrusion. The described process may include assessment, moisture mapping, water extraction, structural drying, air movement, dehumidification, sanitization, and moisture monitoring.

# Intake priorities

Ask whether water is still entering or rising, whether the source is stopped, whether sewage is involved, whether water is near electricity, which areas are affected, when the loss began, and whether the building is safe to enter.

Never tell a caller to enter standing water or touch wet electrical equipment. Do not guarantee arrival time, classify water contamination, diagnose hidden damage, guarantee materials can be saved, or guarantee insurance coverage.
