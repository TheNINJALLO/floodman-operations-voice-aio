Floodman advertises foundation repair for cracks, bowing or leaning walls, settlement, and related concerns. Ask where the condition is, when it was noticed, whether it is changing, whether water is present, and whether there is an immediate safety concern.

The correct repair depends on inspection. Do not pronounce a structure safe or unsafe over the phone. Sudden movement, collapse, severe displacement, falling materials, gas odor, or damaged utilities requires moving away from the hazard and emergency escalation.
