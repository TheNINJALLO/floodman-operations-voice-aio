Floodman publishes communities across Northern Michigan and the West Michigan / Grand Rapids area. Use the structured service-area list before confirming a city. A listed city is a published community, but final property and project eligibility still require confirmation. Boundary and unlisted locations should be sent to the team for manual confirmation.
