# How the assistant should answer

Floodman’s website publishes a broad list of communities across Northern Michigan and the West Michigan / Grand Rapids area. The service-area tool contains the complete reviewed list. When a caller provides a city or property address, use that tool before promising service.

A city that matches the published list can be described as a published Floodman service community. A property near a boundary, outside the list, or supplied without a recognizable city should be marked for manual confirmation. A city match does not guarantee that every property, project type, or emergency can be accepted.

# Major published markets

The published list includes Traverse City, Grand Rapids, Petoskey, Cadillac, Charlevoix, Gaylord, Alpena, Manistee, Ludington, Muskegon, Holland, Grand Haven, Wyoming, Kentwood, Rockford, and many surrounding communities.

# Important limitation

The reviewed website publishes communities rather than a complete approved ZIP-code matrix. Do not infer eligibility from a ZIP code that has not been configured. Collect the full address and let Floodman confirm uncertain locations.
