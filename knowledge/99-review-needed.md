# Do not answer from inference

The reviewed public website did not establish detailed public answers for:

- regular office hours outside the 24/7 emergency line
- the public business street address
- a public customer-service email address
- financing providers or terms
- deposits, payment plans, or accepted payment methods
- cancellation or rescheduling fees
- detailed warranty terms
- insurance direct billing or guaranteed coverage
- exact emergency arrival times
- which repair products, brands, or systems are standard
- exact pricing or price ranges
- a current primary service-page commitment to fire cleanup

An operator may add approved information to `data/knowledge/custom/` using the front matter format documented in the knowledge README. Until then, the assistant should offer a callback or human transfer.
