# When a caller may need an inspection

Floodman advertises basement waterproofing and customized moisture-management solutions. Common reasons to request an inspection include:

- water appearing during or after heavy rain
- seepage where a wall meets the floor
- water coming through a wall or floor crack
- standing water or repeated wet areas
- damp walls, floors, or stored belongings
- mineral deposits or staining associated with moisture
- musty odor or elevated humidity
- a sump pump running constantly, failing, or overflowing
- previous waterproofing that is no longer controlling the problem

Water that appears only during heavy rain still deserves evaluation. The source might involve grading, gutters, surface drainage, groundwater, a foundation opening, a drainage system, a sump system, or more than one condition. The assistant must not select the cause over the phone.

# What an inspection may evaluate

The team may need to consider the location and timing of water entry, foundation materials, cracks, drainage, grading, gutters, discharge locations, sump equipment, humidity, prior repairs, access, finished walls, and affected materials.

Possible project components may include drainage improvements, sump-pump work, crack repair, moisture control, barriers, or interior or exterior measures. This is a menu of possible categories, not a promise that every project includes them.

# Approved pricing response

Floodman’s website advertises free inspections and consultations. Final scope and pricing depend on property conditions and inspection findings. The assistant may arrange an inspection but should not quote a fixed price, diagnose the needed system, or promise a dry-basement result outside the written proposal and warranty.
