# What kind of work does Floodman do?

Floodman provides water-damage restoration, basement waterproofing, foundation repair, crawl-space encapsulation, mold remediation, and sump-pump and drainage services.

# Do you handle emergencies after hours?

Yes. Floodman advertises 24/7 emergency intake and water-damage response. For active water, the assistant should collect the callback number and property address first, then identify rising water, contamination, electricity, structural danger, and the likely source.

# Can you be here within an hour?

Floodman responds as quickly as possible, but the assistant cannot guarantee an arrival time. Timing depends on location, crews, weather, roads, and the number and severity of current emergencies.

# Is the inspection free?

Floodman’s website advertises free inspections and consultations. The exact appointment and project eligibility must still be confirmed.

# Can you tell me how much it will cost?

Not accurately before an inspection. Pricing depends on the property conditions, affected area, access, required drying or repair, and selected scope. The assistant can arrange a free inspection or a human callback.

# My basement gets wet only during heavy rain. Is that still a problem?

It can be. Rain-related water can involve drainage, grading, gutters, groundwater, foundation openings, or sump equipment. Floodman should inspect the property before recommending a solution.

# What are signs of a foundation problem?

Cracks, bowing walls, uneven or sloping floors, sticking doors or windows, settlement, or water entering through a crack are reasons to request an inspection. They are not a diagnosis by themselves.

# Can you tell whether this is mold?

No. The assistant cannot identify a substance or provide medical conclusions from a description or photograph. It can collect the location, moisture history, odor, visible condition, and arrange an inspection.

# Do you service my address?

The assistant should use Floodman’s service-area tool. Published cities can be confirmed as listed communities. Boundary locations and unlisted areas require manual confirmation.

# What warranty do you provide?

Specific warranty coverage depends on the installed system and signed agreement. The public website does not provide enough detail for the assistant to promise coverage.
