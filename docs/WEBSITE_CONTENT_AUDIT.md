# Floodman.com Content Audit for Voice Operations

Reviewed: August 12, 2026

Primary sources:

- `https://floodman.com/`
- `https://floodman.com/blog`
- `https://floodman.com/referrals`

## Approved company facts

The public site supports the main phone number `231-935-4921`, 24/7 emergency water intake, free inspections/consultations, the six principal service groups in the knowledge pack, a published Michigan community list, and a website claim of more than 50 years serving Traverse City and Northern Michigan.

## Approved detailed processes

The homepage describes water-response moisture detection, extraction, structural drying, dehumidification, and antimicrobial treatment. It describes mold containment, negative air pressure, material removal when required, HEPA vacuuming, structural treatment, moisture-source correction, and documentation.

## Educational-only material

Location-targeted blog articles contain useful explanations of symptoms, moisture, waterproofing, crawl-space barriers, and restoration concepts. They are not treated as promises of Floodman’s exact scope, product, warranty, or result.

## Marketing claims converted into safe language

The site contains aggressive phrases such as “permanent solutions,” exact or near-exact emergency response timing, and testimonials naming a repair method. The voice pack does not repeat those as guarantees. It states that scope, arrival, performance, and warranty depend on the property, availability, and written agreement.

## Not established by the reviewed public site

- ordinary office hours
- public street address
- public customer-service email
- financing terms
- deposits or payment methods
- cancellation fees
- detailed warranties
- insurance direct billing
- guaranteed arrival times
- exact pricing
- a current primary service-page commitment to fire cleanup

These topics remain human-review questions until Floodman supplies approved internal information.

## Conflicting or stale page excluded

A separate `https://floodman.com/index.html` chat page exposed a placeholder `(555) 555-5555` emergency number. It conflicts with the main live site and referral page, which publish `231-935-4921`, so the placeholder page was excluded from the knowledge pack.
